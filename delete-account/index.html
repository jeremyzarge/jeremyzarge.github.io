Delete your account

ViteMeals (the website at vitemeals.com and the iOS and Android apps)

You can ask us to delete your ViteMeals account and its data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email jeremyzarge@gmail.com from the email address you sign in to ViteMeals with, with the subject "Delete my ViteMeals account".
  2. We'll confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days, usually much sooner.
Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account and your profile: name, email, apartment, what you can bring, allergies and dietary preferences, availability and settings.
  • Your OneTable connection and notification tokens.
  • Your friend connections and pending friend and apartment requests.
  • Your messages in meals, and your spot in upcoming meals.

What may be kept

So other members' meal history stays accurate, past meals you attended keep an anonymous entry that shows as "Deleted user", with no name or other details. Technical logs are removed automatically within 30 days. If you hosted an upcoming meal with other guests, we'll ask you to hand it to a co-host or cancel it first.
