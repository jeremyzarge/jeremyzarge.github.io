Privacy Policy

ViteMeals (the website at vitemeals.com and the iOS and Android apps) · Last updated November 18, 2026

ViteMeals is a small app for friends to plan shared meals: who's hosting, who's coming and who's bringing what. This policy explains what information it collects, how that information is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address from the Google or Apple account you sign in with, and an internal account number.
  • Profile: the name you enter, your apartment, what you can bring, food allergies and dietary preferences, your weekly lunch and dinner availability, and your notification and tracking settings.
  • Activity in the app: meals you create or join, what you're bringing, meal messages, friend connections and apartment memberships.
  • Notifications: a device token (app) or browser push subscription (website) so we can send you notifications.
  • OneTable connection (optional): if you connect OneTable, we store your OneTable access token so we can sync your meals and reservations with OneTable for you.
  • Technical logs: basic events and error reports (for example "meal created" or a sign-in error) with your account number, used to fix problems.

Allergy and dietary information can reveal health details. You choose whether to add it, and it is used only to help hosts plan food.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show meals, coordinate food, connect friends and apartments, and send the notifications you've turned on.
  • To sync with OneTable, only if you connect it.
  • To draft OneTable event descriptions when you ask: the theme words and meal title you enter are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to write a draft.
  • To keep the app working and secure, and to fix bugs.

We don't sell your information, show ads, or use your information for advertising.

Who can see your information

  • Other ViteMeals members who are signed in can see your name, apartment, what you can bring, your allergies and dietary preferences, and your participation in meals they can see. Your weekly availability follows your Status Visibility setting.
  • Only you can read your email address and your OneTable token.
  • Service providers that run the app for us, each handling data only to provide their service: Google Firebase (sign-in, database and hosting), Cloudflare (website delivery and our server functions), Apple and Google push notification services, OneTable (if you connect it), and Anthropic (only for descriptions you ask it to draft).
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Security

Every request requires signing in, and server-side database rules control who can read or write each piece of data. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). It is not end-to-end encrypted.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account exists. Technical logs are deleted automatically after 30 days. You can delete your account and its data at any time; see Delete your account.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your profile details, allergies and preferences in Edit Profile.
  • Turn notifications on or off in Notifications, or in your device settings.
  • Disconnect OneTable at any time in Edit Profile.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing us.

Children

ViteMeals is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
